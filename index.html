Your first 90 days — a step-by-step rollout plan

Adopting the policy is step one. This simple timeline shows what to do next to put it into practice — and who is responsible for each step.

  • Week 1
    Choose one person to own AI decisions (the “AI Owner”) and send this policy to all staff.
    Done by: Leadership
  • Week 2
    Make a list of every AI tool people actually use — including free ones they signed up for themselves.
    Done by: AI Owner
  • Week 3
    Note where personal data is kept and how it is used, as the UAE data-protection law (PDPL) requires.
    Done by: AI Owner
  • Week 6
    Hold a short (45-minute) session so staff understand the rules and how to follow them.
    Done by: HR
  • 6 months
    Revisit and update the policy as laws or your AI use change.
    Done by: AI Owner

    ⏳

    The regulatory lag

    Early on, AI is easy to shape but harms are unclear; later, harms are clear but change is hard (Collingridge, 1980). A company policy can act faster than law.

    ⚠️

    Three risks we create

    Policy theatre (adopted, never followed); accountability (who owns a wrong draft?); shared blind spots (one omission copied widely).

    🎯

    Our response

    A named AI Owner, 90-day actions, a 6-month review, and a clear "not legal advice" notice on every output.
